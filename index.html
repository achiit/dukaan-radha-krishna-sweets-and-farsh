Our story

A neighbourhood counter, not a chain.

We started this shop because Santacruz East deserved a place where you could pick up a box of sweets for the family and a hot plate of vada pav for yourself, without walking to two different shops.

The sweets follow recipes I learned at home from my elders. Peda is stirred slowly, barfi is cut while it is still fresh, and the Bengali sweets are made the way they are meant to be: soft and not too sugary. The street food side is the one I keep an eye on every evening, because that is when the lane fills up.

Come in, taste something, and tell us what you thought. Half our menu came from customer suggestions.
